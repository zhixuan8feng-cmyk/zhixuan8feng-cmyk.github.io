I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at North Carolina State University.

My research focuses on Financial Econometrics, High-Frequency Data, and Time Series Econometrics. In particular, my dissertation investigates high-dimensional realized covariance dynamics, regime-switching structures, and latent state inference using Realized GARCH frameworks.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market and available for interviews.

Research

High-Dimensional Covariance Dynamics & Latent State Inference (Job Market Paper)
with Denis [Denis的全名]
Abstract: This paper develops a two-stage Hidden Markov Model with a block-structure specification to capture structural shifts in high-dimensional return-volatility dynamics while avoiding the curse of dimensionality. We construct daily positive semi-definite covariance matrices using refreshing-time and multivariate realized kernels, and implement the Hamilton Filter and Louis Formula for recursive state inference and robust parameter estimation within a Realized GARCH framework.
